OUR STORY

A café, bakery, and pastry shop by the sea

Sitting on the terrace at Kroštola, literally a few steps above sea level, offers an experience that's hard to replicate elsewhere in Koper. More than just a classic café, it blends a bakery, a pastry shop, and all-day dining — breakfast, brunch, and light lunch alongside more than 20 different fresh cakes daily, including local favorites like kremšnita and gibanica.
